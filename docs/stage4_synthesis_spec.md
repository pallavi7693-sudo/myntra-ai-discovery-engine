## 2. Opportunity Scoring Formula (Edge Case 18.1)

To prevent high-frequency minor feedback from outranking critical purchase friction, opportunities are ranked using the weighted formula:

$$\text{Opportunity Score} = \text{Frequency (\%)} \times \text{Friction Severity Weight}$$

### Friction Severity Weights

| Friction / Barrier Category | Severity Weight | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Return Concern / Exchange Policy** | `1.5` | High monetary risk & post-purchase anxiety trigger |
| **Delivery Delay / Tracking Friction** | `1.4` | High occasion abandonment risk |
| **Price / Discount Delay** | `1.3` | Direct purchase delay driver |
| **Quality / Fabric Uncertainty** | `1.2` | Trust & value mismatch |
| **Lack of Reviews / Social Proof** | `1.1` | Confidence gap |
| **Size / Fit Uncertainty** | `1.1` | Primary return trigger |

## 3. Mandatory Causal Language Restrictions (Edge Case 17.1)
- **Prohibited Phrases:** `"will increase conversion by X%"`, `"causes users to abandon"`, `"guarantees X% revenue growth"`.
- **Enforced Phrasing:** `"appeared in X% of hesitation conversations"`, `"is associated with"`, `"represents a potential purchase barrier"`, `"may reduce friction"`.
