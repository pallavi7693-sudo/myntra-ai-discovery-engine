QUESTION

"What prevents wishlisted fashion products from being purchased on Myntra?"

--------------------------------------------------

EXECUTIVE INSIGHT

Based on Myntra-scoped customer review analysis (701 purchase-hesitation conversations), the primary observed friction factors preventing wishlisted products from converting into purchases on Myntra are Price & Discount Delays (35.2%), Delivery Delay Concerns (24.7%), Product Quality Uncertainty (24.7%), and Return Policy Concerns (19.3%). Cross-platform reviews (AJIO & Nykaa) were analyzed for general wishlist intent.

--------------------------------------------------

QUANTIFIED FINDINGS

   - Quality & Fabric Uncertainty: 173 / 701 (24.7%)
   - Return/Exchange Policy Concern: 135 / 701 (19.3%)
